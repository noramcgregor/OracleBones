Oracle Bones: The OG ChatGPT

How the Shang Dynasty asked questions 3,000 years ago

These bones are the oldest items we have at the British Library, they come from China in the times of the Shang Dynasty 1600–1050 BC!

Oracle bones are animal bones, usually ox shoulder bones (like this one!) or the underside of turtle shells that were used by priests to ask questions to the gods for the Shang kings.

They would carve questions (about things like the weather, the future of the dynasty or military victory) onto the bone or shell using a sharp tool.

A hot metal rod was then applied to the bone or shell until it cracked. The priests would then interpret the cracks and carve the answer on them!

Caring for objects like this one — so they can be seen by everyone — takes a whole team of people with all sorts of different skills!
